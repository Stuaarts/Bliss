# Product vision

Bliss helps customers discover local service providers or describe work and receive
quotes. A provider can be an individual or a company with members. One account may
both buy and provide services. Reliability and transparent booking history matter
more than a large collection of disconnected screens.

The portfolio demonstrates Java 21, Quarkus, PostgreSQL/Flyway, durable human-in-the-loop
workflows, Flutter, OIDC, authorization and tests. Real payments, public deployment,
KYC and legal/commercial policies are separately approved beta work.
